How the numbers work

  • Estimated rent — from 15 current Bryan–College Station rental comps pulled the same day (Zillow Rentals, Apartments.com, Craigslist): bedroom medians 1BR $857 · 2BR $1,049 · 3BR $1,648 · 4BR $1,748, cross-checked at ~$1.15/sq ft. The lower of the two figures is used, so estimates lean conservative. This week's comp sample skews cheaper than sweep #1's, which moved several verdicts — treat single-week rent-model swings with care.
  • Expenses — the 50% rule from Real Estate by the Numbers: operating expenses (taxes, insurance, maintenance, vacancy, management) estimated at 50% of gross rent, before debt service. Texas property taxes are high; verify each property's actual tax bill before acting.
  • Financing — 20% down, 7.0% 30-year fixed; cash invested = 23% of price (down payment + ~3% closing costs).
  • The screen — MEETS SCREEN = rent ≥ 1% of price per month AND cash flow ≥ $100/mo AND cash-on-cash ≥ 8%. CLOSE = rent/price ≥ 0.8% with positive cash flow. Everything else is a PASS at today's price.
  • Caveats — screening estimates only, not investment advice. Condition and rehab costs are not in these numbers; several Bryan/Navasota listings are 1940s–50s stock with long days-on-market, which cuts both ways: negotiable, but inspect hard.
